### FCPX Headstart

Drop in raw interview footage, get a Final Cut Pro project with the strongest answers in story order.

![FCPX Headstart's review page: the chosen passages in story order, the player and timeline, and the Tightness setting](/static/fcpx-headstart.jpg)

FCPX Headstart is a Mac app for editors who cut interviews. It transcribes your footage on your Mac, an AI picks the strongest answers and puts them in story order, ums, restarts and long pauses are cut, and it writes a Final Cut Pro project with subtitles. Final Cut is the only output. You open it, trim and finish it like any other project. The first hour of an interview edit is done, the rest is yours.

The video files are read in place and are never copied or uploaded. Each piece in the timeline is a normal clip pointing at your original file, so you can trim past the app's cut points.

- **Your AI, four ways:** your own Claude plan through a connector, included Headstart Cloud units, a model running on your Mac, or your own Anthropic key. With your Claude plan only transcript text of the chosen passages goes through, never video.
- **Tightness:** Off, Light, Medium or Tight decides how many pauses, ums, hedges and restarts are cut.
- **Subtitles:** native Final Cut subtitles, also translated, or spoken and translated together.
- **Interview work:** separate mic recordings matched by sound, a B camera stacked above the edit, one clip per chapter for long interviews, and a script mode for scripted retakes that gives you each repeated passage as a Final Cut audition. Cutting ums has only been listened to in English.
- **Checked file:** the FCPXML is checked against Apple's definition and tested with Final Cut Pro 12.
