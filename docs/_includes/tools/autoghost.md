### AutoGhost

**Drop in your camera and audio files. Get back a finished multicam episode.**

AutoGhost is a Mac app that edits multicam interviews and podcasts for you. It syncs the cameras by sound, works out who is speaking (even when everyone is on one audio track), cuts to the right camera, removes long pauses and evens out the voices.

With **AutoGhost Studio** you can then add name graphics, your logo, intro, outro, breaks and music, fix any cut by hand, and export the finished video, horizontal or vertical. Prefer to finish in Final Cut Pro? AutoGhost hands you the edit as a multicam project instead.

- Learns a regular show's style from one to three finished episodes, then edits new ones the same way.
- Works in English and Arabic, including right-to-left name graphics.
- Everything runs on your Mac. No uploads.
