### octuedit

**AI video editor for Mac that hands the edit to Final Cut Pro.**

octuedit is an AI video editor for Mac. Drop in your footage and describe the edit in plain words, and your own Claude Code or Codex cuts it on a real timeline: pauses, filler words and retakes removed, captions, vertical reframes, and multicam podcasts synced by sound.

When you want to finish by hand, export the edit as an FCPXML project and open it in Final Cut Pro with the cuts, chapter markers and chosen camera angles in place. Footage and transcription stay on your Mac. $99 once, macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
