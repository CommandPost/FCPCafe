### Reelary

**Search your footage by describing the shot. Runs on your Mac, no upload.**

Reelary indexes the video and photo folders you point it at and lets you search every frame in plain language, for example "drone shot over a beach at sunset". The vision model, the footage and the index all stay on your machine, and search works offline once the model is downloaded.

From the results you can lay out a script-driven timeline, one segment per line of your script, then export it as an FCPXML project for Final Cut Pro (or EDL for other NLEs) that references your original files. The free version has unlimited indexing and search; NLE export is in Pro, a one-time purchase that covers Mac and Windows.
