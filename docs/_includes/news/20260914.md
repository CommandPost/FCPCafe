### [14th September 2026](/news/20260914)

![](/static/production-data-cover-banner.png)

**Vigneswaran Rajkumar**, the creator of [Marker Data](https://markerdata.theacharya.co), has released Production Data, a lightweight spreadsheet creation application crafted for Final Cut Pro users seeking a straightforward, native way to turn timeline metadata into structured production reports. It is now available on the Mac App Store for USD $5.99, with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

You can download and learn more on the [Production Data website](https://productiondata.theacharya.co).
